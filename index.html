Selected work

SimLingo × Euro Truck Simulator 2

A fine-tuned InternVL2-1B vision-language-action model that drives a truck in Euro Truck Simulator 2 from screen pixels. I profiled each stage of the pipeline and reduced inference latency from 691 ms to 57 ms per frame on a single consumer GPU.

The report also covers the approaches that did not help: INT8 and FP8 quantization, TensorRT, and a dual-GPU setup.
