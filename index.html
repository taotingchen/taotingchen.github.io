I research vision-language-action models for autonomous driving, with a focus on real-time inference.

About

I am a Ph.D. student at the Institute of Computer Science and Engineering, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU), advised by Prof. Ching-Chun Huang.

My research is on end-to-end planning for autonomous driving with vision-language-action models, and on making these models fast enough to run in real time on edge hardware.

PyTorch · VLM / VLA · NAVSIM · CARLA · Bench2Drive · Token pruning · Neural architecture search · NVIDIA Jetson
